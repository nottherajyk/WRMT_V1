# Brag Plan: wherearemytools

## What is this app?
An unapologetically neo-brutalist, all-in-one suite of 50+ free browser-based online tools for image conversion, PDF editing, social media downloading, and text manipulation with zero signup.

## The angle
"Why load 50 bloated SaaS apps when one loud, neon, no-signup toolbox can do it all right in your browser?"
A fast-paced, high-energy launch video highlighting the sheer variety and instant browser-based power with a bold Starface Neo-Brutalist visual identity.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A giant pitch-black card slams onto a bright neon yellow background:
"WHERE ARE MY TOOLS?"
Followed by a fast neon pink badge popping in: "Found them. All 50+ of them."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Category Explosion**: Rapid sequential reveal of the 4 core tool pills: Image, PDF, Social, Text with their custom neon accent colors (#00D2FF, #16C641, #FF007F, #FFB000).
- **Tool Showcase / Flow**:
  - Image Conversion & Compression: Drag-and-drop instant conversion without uploading to a server.
  - PDF Suite: Merge, split, encrypt, compress, and edit PDFs locally.
  - Social Media & Video: YouTube, Instagram, Spotify tools built-in.
- **Zero Friction Guarantee**: Big bold stamp: "100% Free • No Signup • Zero Ads • Browser Local".

## Outro / punchline
"Stop searching. Start building."
Logo & URL reveal: **wherearemytools.com** with a neo-brutalist shadow pop.

## User flow worth showing
1. Select a tool (e.g. Image Converter or PDF Merge) from the category grid.
2. Drop files -> instant processing locally.
3. Download result immediately.

## Tone
- Preset: `default` (Playful, clean, neo-brutalist postable energy)
- Creative direction: Loud Neo-Brutalist Show & Tell
- Interpretation: Fast-paced entrance, sharp offsets, high-contrast neon palette, punchy bold fonts, zero corporate fluff.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#FAFF05` (Starface Neon Yellow)
- Primary Accent: `#FF007F` (Neon Hot Pink)
- Secondary Accents: `#00D2FF` (Sky Blue), `#16C641` (Green), `#FFB000` (Honey Orange)
- Text / Borders: `#000000` (Pitch Black with 6px flat offset shadows)
- Display font: `Fredoka`, sans-serif
- Body font: `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: High-contrast neo-brutalist cards, solid offset shadows (`6px 6px 0px #000000`), rounded pill badges (`var(--radius-full)`).

## Share copy (draft)
50+ free online tools for images, PDFs, social media & text. Zero signup, 100% browser-local. Try wherearemytools today! ⚡

## Audio direction
- Role: Upbeat energetic neo-brutalist pop/tech bed
- Music: Energetic upbeat bed
- SFX posture: Moderate, motion-matched pop/click UI sounds for card reveals
- Audio-coupled moments: Tool pills popping in sequence, bold text slams, button click on CTA.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 3.5s
- Neon Yellow background (#FAFF05).
- Giant pitch-black card slams down with 10px offset shadow: **"WHERE ARE MY TOOLS?"**
- Neon Pink badge pops in: **"50+ Tools. Zero Signup."**
- Sequential/interaction: Card slams, badge pops.
- Transition mood: Clean hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Category Grid & Tool Power — 5.0s
- Grid of 4 high-contrast neo-brutalist cards popping in sequentially:
  1. 🖼️ **IMAGE** — Convert, Compress, Crop (#00D2FF)
  2. 📄 **PDF** — Merge, Split, Protect (#16C641)
  3. 📱 **SOCIAL** — YouTube, Insta, Spotify (#FF007F)
  4. ✍️ **TEXT** — Password Gen, Counter (#FFB000)
- Sequential/interaction: Cards pop in 1-by-1 with offset shadow drops.
- Transition mood: Slide wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Browser-Local Speed Flow — 5.0s
- A simulated browser window showing a dropzone:
  - File `my-image.png` dropped in.
  - Progress bar fills instantly (0 → 100%).
  - Badge appears: **"100% Client-Side • Private & Fast"**
- Sequential/interaction: Drag drop simulation + progress bar fill + badge pop.
- Transition mood: Fast crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro / Call to Action — 4.5s
- Full-screen neo-brutalist banner.
- Big text: **"Stop searching. Start tools."**
- Main URL card pops in with heavy black shadow: **wherearemytools**
- Subtext: "Free forever. Bookmark now."
- Transition mood: Hold to end.

**Music mood for this video:** Upbeat / Energetic Neo-Brutalist
